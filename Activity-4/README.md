Activity Title: Group Presentation and Exam

An activity in which I as part of a group had to create a presentation focused on google sheets. The exam included other google applications like google forms and google docs. 

What have I learned?:

With this activity I was able to learn a lot actually in how to use google sheets, in both practical and creative ways. Like how to use it for budget tables and how to add designs to them. A challenge for me was remembering all the different commands you can use. I think what I learned however will be really helpful in the future in case I have a business and want to keep track of stock, budgeting, or sales.
